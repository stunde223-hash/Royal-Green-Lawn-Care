Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current Royal Green Lawn Care website. Contact: oluwafemiemmanuel@gmail.com.

Information you choose to share

This website has no account registration or payment checkout. The yard care enquiry form prepares an email on your device. Form entries are not submitted to a server or saved by this site. Choosing to open the email draft passes the message to your email application; you must send it there. The copy button copies your prepared message to your device clipboard when permitted. When you contact the business by email, phone, or Facebook, you may share contact details, an address, yard photographs, and service requirements. These details are used to respond to your inquiry and coordinate requested services.

Hosting and external services

The site is prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. Fonts are hosted with this website; no external font requests are made. Facebook links take you to a separate service governed by Meta’s policies.

Cookies and analytics

This version does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or website cookies. Linked third-party services may use their own cookies.

Your requests

Contact the business using the email above to ask about information you have shared or request correction or deletion, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information with your yard inquiry.
